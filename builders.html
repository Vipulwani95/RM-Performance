Leak 1: 66.3% Volume Lost in Expired & NC
799 Auto-Expired leads and 669 Uncontactable (RNR) leads make up 1,468 lost opportunities.

Actionable Solution:
  • Enforce a strict 5-minute SLA for initial dialling after lead creation.
  • Deploy an automated WhatsApp Bot / IVR confirmation instantly upon lead capture.
  • Introduce a scheduled alternate-time reachout for unreached numbers.
Leak 2: Site Visit Cancellation Rate (66.7%)
Out of 48 Site Visits booked, only 16 were completed while 24 were cancelled and 8 are pending.

Actionable Solution:
  • Provide NoBroker-sponsored cab pickup/drop service for confirmed site visits.
  • Send WhatsApp calendar reminders with direct location links and RM contact at T-24h and T-2h.
  • Engage the buyer with a curated project video walkthrough 12 hours prior to the visit.
Strength: High Call Patch Conversion (80%)
When an RM successfully completes a 3-way Call Patch pitch (60 leads), 80% (48 leads) agree to schedule a Site Visit!

Actionable Solution:
  • Double down on getting spoken leads onto instant call patches with builder project specialists.
  • Incentivize tele-calling RMs specifically on Call Patch patch-through rates.
  • Replicate Bangalore and Pune's pitch playbooks across Chennai and Mumbai.
Leak 4: Real-Time Visibility, Univw API & Follow-up Gaps
Key Pipeline Leaks & Technical Gaps:
  • Visibility Real-Time Issue: Real-time visibility and status updates on forwarded leads are not provided back by the builders on-ground team.
  • 20%–30% Leads Missing in Univw: Tech issue / API integration failure causes 20% to 30% of leads not getting created in the Univw CRM.
  • Follow-up Latency: Lack of prompt action on warm leads provided by Ambassador Club Relationship Managers.
Actionable Solution:
  • Fix the Univw API integration failure to ensure 100% automated lead ingestion with instant failure alerts.
  • Establish a shared real-time tracking interface with the builders team for live status, feedback, and visit tracking.
  • Enforce a strict 15-minute follow-up SLA on RM-provided leads with automated escalation to city managers.
